N1	The Endless Tower arrives, along with the Librarian and the game wiki, lots of melee balance and more optimization for mobile.
N2	Endless Tower
N3	The Endless Tower: the Tower Guardian, west of the Prontera square, opens the gate to characters with a 2nd job (earned through the Prontera Castle story). Floors last one minute, with monsters that never stop and a minimum score you need to keep climbing. Every 10 floors the scenery turns into another stage, and floors 10, 20, 30… are MVP floors. At the start you pick a Deity that ranks up every 10 floors, and before each floor an upgrade card chosen from your equipped skills and your basic attack (epic after floors 5 and 10 of each block). Nothing drops inside: the tower points from every run buy permanent upgrades that only count there. You can save a run once and continue it another day. There is a monthly ladder, overall and per 2nd job. It needs a registered account: the server checks every run and its result is the one that counts. It is still in testing, so the numbers may change.
N4	Accounts
N5	Up to 15 characters per account (was 10): the character select has one more row.
N6	Balance
N7	Swordsman, Thief and Merchant: their area skills deal 40% more to bosses.
N8	Less HP on two MVPs: Golden Thief Bug 58,000 (was 67,500) and Sunken Ship Drake 180,000 (was 200,000).

N10	Sword Dance grows out from you and opens in three pulses, each one reaching further: no more hole in the middle, and whatever is close to you takes all three. Extra pulses from cards fit in the same opening. 9 s cooldown (was 13).
N11	Crushing Bash with extra targets: spreads its slams to reach as many different monsters as possible, instead of hitting several that are stuck together.
N12	Thief: Throw Stone, Fan of Knives and Caltrops scale with STR (melee ATK) like the rest of its skills, instead of ranged ATK (DEX).
N13	Swordsman and Merchant deal more damage with melee basic attacks: 75% more with a one-handed weapon and 125% more with a two-handed one. The Thief, 35% and 50% more.
N14	Bash deals much more damage from the start (300 at level 1, was 120) and grows little per level (+3%): 363 at level 8 (was 170).
N15	Mammonite deals 15% more damage at level 1 (150) and each level adds a bit less (+15%).
N16	Envenom's poison deals 150% more damage.
N17	Wandering Shadow (Thief Shadows tree): after Shadowstep's last jump, your basic attacks keep dealing 2000% of their damage for 3 s.
N18	Two-Hand Quicken (Swordsman Fury tree) gives +10 Haste per rank to basic attacks (was +6).
N19	Berserk (Swordsman Fury tree) also gives +20% melee reach and +10 Haste on basic attacks.
N20	Fan of Knives throws two fans in a row from level 1 (was one).
N21	Ranged basic attacks (bow, gun, huuma, rod and instrument) and Arrow Repel can only gain +3 projectiles from in-run cards, and those cards are always white (+1 projectile). Deity, talents and gear add on top.
N22	The Mage's infused attack no longer gets +projectile cards. Lightning Infusion now gives +2 projectiles (was +1).
N23	Pierce is now a chance, and a projectile pierces at most 1 enemy (2 in total). Pierce talents give 20% across all their points: Ice Infusion and Piercing Arrow give 20%; 2-point talents give 10% per point. Cards give 1% (white) to 5% (orange) and the projectile Deity gives 10%. True Sight adds 10% while it lasts.
N24	Skills that pierce everything (Arrow Repel, Huuma, Shield Bash…) are unchanged. Skills that pierce by default (Throw Shuriken) now always pierce exactly 1 enemy, no more.
N25	Chests no longer offer upgrades your character can't use: melee reach only shows up if your basic attack or one of your skills hits in melee, and poison, burn and bleed damage (Damage over Time Elixir and Deity of Torment) only shows up once you have poisoned, burned or made an enemy bleed during the run.
N26	Sword Dance Deity: the weapon stays charged with lightning for 5 s after the dance (was 2 s).
N27	Frost Diver Deity: Frozen Burst deals 35% of the frozen enemy's max HP to those around it (was 15%), plus 1% per refine.
